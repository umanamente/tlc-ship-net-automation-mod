# Artwork

The final Blender masters for Basic and Advanced are in [3d_models](../3d_models/). Their mesh exports and this folder's catalogue icons and printed-marking atlas feed the [asset pipeline](../docs/maintainers/asset-pipeline.md).

- [Catalogue icons](catalog/) are in-game construction menu inputs.
- [Printed markings](models/winch-markings.png) are baked from the Blender masters.
- [GitHub artwork](branding/github/) contains the README banner and in-game screenshots of both installed controllers.
- [Model renders](models/) show the standalone Basic and Advanced controllers.
- The mod cover and screenshots are available in the [Nexus Mods gallery](https://www.nexusmods.com/thelastcaretaker/mods/454?tab=images).

The renders show the mod models. Keep the editable sources with their generated assets.
